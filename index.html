SMARTER
COMPUTER CARE.

Simple, practical tips to keep in mind as a laptop or desktop user. For a diagnosis or repair decision, consult a qualified technician.

01 / Practical guidance

EIGHT SMART
HABITS.

Helpful general information, not a diagnosis or a substitute for hands-on assessment.

TIP / 01

Back up important files

Keep a separate backup of photos, documents and other important data. Test that your backup can be opened before you need it.

TIP / 02

Keep vents unobstructed

Use a computer on a clean, stable surface and avoid blocking its air vents. Stop using a device that smells burnt or becomes unusually hot.

TIP / 03

Install trusted updates

Use built-in operating-system tools and trusted vendors for updates. Avoid suspicious download links and unknown “fix” utilities.

TIP / 04

Look out for phishing

Do not share passwords or one-time codes with callers claiming to be technical support. Verify requests independently.

TIP / 05

Notice early warning signs

Frequent crashes, unexplained noises or repeated startup failures can mean it is time to ask for help.

TIP / 06

Secure accounts and devices

Use unique passwords, enable multifactor authentication where available and lock the screen when stepping away.

TIP / 07

Be careful with liquids

If liquid spills on a powered device, stop using it and seek professional guidance rather than repeatedly turning it on.

TIP / 08

Ask before replacing parts

Confirm compatibility, costs and the data implications of any recommended hardware changes.

02 / Safety reminder

When should you stop troubleshooting?

If you notice smoke, a strong burning smell, a swollen battery or other signs of physical danger, stop using the device, disconnect it from power if safe, and do not attempt to open or charge it. Ask a qualified professional for safe next steps.

For less urgent problems, make a note of symptoms and contact RR COMPUTER to ask about available services before visiting.
